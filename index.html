/Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between formal and informal risk mapping?
Formal risk mapping relies on traditional banking data, CRB scores, and registered corporate financials. Informal risk mapping uses alternative data points like mobile money flows, vendor payment histories, and community vouching to accurately assess the creditworthiness of unbanked actors.
How secure is the collateral registry?
Our collateral registry employs bank-grade encryption and immutable audit logs. Once an asset is registered and tied to a credit facility, it cannot be double-pledged across any of our participating formal or informal lending partners.
Can SMEs use CrediSafe directly?
Yes. CrediSafe is built specifically to bridge the gap. SMEs can log their vendor financing, issue digital receipts, and track repayment flows entirely through our WhatsApp integration without needing complex desktop software.
What forms of ID are accepted for KYC?
We support automated verification for Zimbabwean National IDs, valid Passports, and Driver's Licenses. For corporate entities, we require CR14/CR6 documents, Certificates of Incorporation, and valid tax clearance certificates for comprehensive KYB vetting.
